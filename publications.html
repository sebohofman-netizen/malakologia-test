Publications of the Association of Polish Malacologists

This page contains information about the most important publications of the Association of Polish Malacologists.

The Association of Polish Malacologists is a publisher of Folia Malacologica, the only malacological journal in Poland. Original articles are published quarterly in English and are available on-line. The journal is scored by the Ministry of Education and Science (20 points).

Folia Malacologica is covered by Web of Science in Emerging Sources Citation Index (ESCI), Biological Abstracts, BIOSIS Previews and Zoological Record. Folia Malacologica is also indexed by Scopus, DOAJ, AGRO, EBSCO and ICI Journals Master List (Index Copernicus).

We encourage you to publish your research results in Folia Malacologica.
