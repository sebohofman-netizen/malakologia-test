Statute of the Association of Polish Malacologists

§ 1

The Association shall bear the name Association of Polish Malacologists, hereinafter referred to as the APM.

§ 2

  1. The area of activity of the APM shall be the territory of the Republic of Poland.
  2. The seat of the central authorities of the APM shall be located in Poznań.

§ 3

The APM is a registered association operating under the applicable law on associations and, as such, possesses legal personality.

§ 4

The APM may become a member of domestic and foreign associations with the same or a similar scope of activity.

§ 5

  1. The APM shall use a circular seal bearing the inscription “Association of Polish Malacologists”.
  2. The authorities and organisational units of the APM shall use rectangular seals, the design of which shall be specified in separate regulations adopted by the Executive Board.
  3. The APM may establish membership badges and commemorative badges.

§ 6

The APM is a scientific and professional association bringing together Polish malacologists in Poland and abroad.

§ 7

The APM shall base its activities on the voluntary work of its members.

§ 8

The objectives of the APM are:

  1. to promote the development of malacology in cooperation with related societies, organisations and institutions in Poland and abroad;
  2. to initiate and support scientific research in malacology and related sciences;
  3. to disseminate knowledge and professional skills among members and supporters of the APM by familiarising them with achievements in malacology and encouraging APM members to engage in research activities;
  4. to support the development of education in malacology, the advancement of scientific staff, and to facilitate contacts between APM members and the international scientific community;
  5. to popularise both established and recent achievements in the knowledge of molluscs among non-scientific audiences, particularly teachers, university students and school pupils;
  6. to represent the interests of APM members in matters related to their scientific and professional activities.

§ 9

The APM pursues its objectives through:

  1. bringing together its members within the organisational structures of the APM;
  2. submitting scientific research projects at regional, national and international levels;
  3. initiating and conducting scientific research independently, in cooperation with, or on behalf of scientific institutions and other organisations;
  4. providing scientific advice and consultancy services at the request of interested institutions, with any resulting income allocated to the statutory objectives of the APM;
  5. organising national and international conferences, symposia and seminars of a specialised and interdisciplinary nature;
  6. organising Congresses of Polish Malacologists for scientific, statutory and organisational purposes;
  7. preparing exhibitions and reviews presenting the scientific achievements of Polish malacology;
  8. publishing scientific, professional and popular-science publications, including its own journal Folia Malacologica;
  9. developing and utilising national and international scientific information in malacology and related sciences, and disseminating information on modern scientific research equipment;
  10. actively participating in environmental protection and promoting public awareness of the need to protect the natural environment;
  11. strengthening scientific, cultural and collegial ties among APM members;
  12. maintaining contacts with foreign malacological associations.

§ 10

The APM has the right to award diplomas, prizes, medals, honorary distinctions and commemorative badges. Such awards shall be granted in accordance with separate regulations, the adoption of which falls within the competence of the Executive Board.

§ 11

The APM may apply for authorisations to prepare assessments, opinions and expert reports in the fields of malacology, palaeozoology, ecology and nature conservation.

§ 12

  1. Membership of the APM may be held by natural persons and legal entities, as well as institutions and organisations without legal personality.
  2. The APM shall comprise ordinary, supporting and honorary members.

§ 13

  1. Any Polish citizen who accepts the objectives and programme of the APM and wishes to participate actively in their implementation may become an ordinary member upon submission of a written declaration endorsed by two sponsoring members and payment of an admission fee equal to one-half of the annual membership fee.
  2. An ordinary member of the APM shall have the right to:
    1. elect and be elected to the authorities of the APM;
    2. participate in the activities of the APM;
    3. obtain advice and assistance in matters covered by the Statute and the programme of the APM;
    4. submit motions and proposals concerning the activities of the APM;
    5. receive information on the directions of activity of the APM;
    6. use items symbolising membership in the APM.
  3. An ordinary member shall be obliged to:
    1. strive to achieve and promote the objectives of the APM;
    2. comply with the provisions of the Statute, regulations and resolutions of the authorities of the APM;
    3. pay membership fees regularly.
  4. A citizen of a foreign country may also become an ordinary member of the APM, provided that they accept the objectives and programme of the APM and wish to participate actively in their implementation, upon submission of a written declaration endorsed by two Polish sponsoring members and payment of an admission fee equal to one-half of the annual membership fee.
  5. Ordinary members who are citizens of foreign countries shall enjoy the same rights and obligations as ordinary members who are Polish citizens, except for the right to stand for election to the authorities of the APM.

§ 14

  1. A legal entity, institution or organisation without legal personality that recognises the objectives of the APM, wishes to support them logistically, and pays a fee equal to ten times the annual membership fee may become a supporting member.
  2. Supporting members shall be admitted by the Executive Board.
  3. A supporting member of the APM has the right to participate in meetings of the authorities of the APM through its representative, who shall have an advisory vote. A supporting member may also benefit from scientific consultation provided by the APM and submit proposals to its authorities.
  4. A supporting member shall be obliged to fulfil declared commitments in a timely manner and to comply with the provisions of the Statute, regulations and resolutions of the authorities of the APM.

§ 15

  1. Any natural person who has rendered outstanding services to the development of malacology may become an honorary member of the APM.
  2. Honorary membership of the APM shall be conferred by the General Meeting of Members upon a motion submitted by the Executive Board, by secret ballot, with a two-thirds majority of votes cast in the presence of at least one-half of the statutory membership.
  3. An honorary member of the APM shall enjoy all the rights of an ordinary member and shall additionally be exempt from the obligation to pay membership fees.

§ 16

  1. Membership in the APM shall cease as a result of:
    1. dissolution of the APM;
    2. withdrawal from the APM submitted in writing;
    3. removal from the register of members due to non-payment of membership fees or declared contributions for a period exceeding 24 months;
    4. removal from the register of members due to a serious violation of the Statute or programme of the APM, or pursuant to a final decision of the Disciplinary Court for actions detrimental to the APM;
    5. dissolution, change in the scope of activity, or loss of legal personality by a supporting member.
  2. A decision of the Executive Board removing a member from the register of members may be appealed to the next General Meeting of Members of the APM, whose decision in this matter shall be final.

§ 17

The APM shall have a unified scientific and organisational structure.

§ 18

The governing bodies of the APM are:

  1. the General Meeting of Members;
  2. the Executive Board;
  3. the Audit Committee;
  4. the Disciplinary Court.

§ 19

The editorial work of Folia Malacologica shall be directed by the Editorial Board, consisting of the Editor-in-Chief, scientific editors, technical editors, and a panel of consultants.

§ 20

  1. The term of office of the governing bodies of the APM shall be four years. Members of the governing bodies shall perform their duties on an honorary basis. The same office may not be held for more than two consecutive terms.
  2. The governing bodies of the APM shall be elected by secret ballot and shall remain in office until the newly elected bodies have been constituted.
  3. Unless otherwise provided in this Statute, resolutions of all governing bodies shall be adopted by a simple majority of votes in the presence of at least one-half of those entitled to vote. In the event of a tie, the chairperson of the meeting shall have the casting vote.
  4. Should the permanent membership of the Executive Board, the Audit Committee or the Disciplinary Court be reduced, vacant positions may be filled by co-option. If co-opted members exceed one-half of the statutory membership of any of these bodies, the Executive Board shall convene an Extraordinary General Meeting of Members. The term of office of newly elected or co-opted members shall expire at the end of the current term of office of the respective body.
  5. The composition of the Editorial Board of Folia Malacologica and of other publications shall be appointed by the Executive Board of the APM, which shall adopt the relevant regulations defining their scope of activity.

A. General Meeting of Members

§ 21

The General Meeting of Members shall be the supreme authority of the APM and may be convened as either an ordinary or an extraordinary meeting.

§ 22

The powers of the Ordinary General Meeting of Members shall include:

  1. determining the programmes and principal directions of the statutory, scientific and financial activities of the APM;
  2. adopting the Statute and amendments thereto;
  3. deciding on the dissolution of the APM and the allocation of its assets;
  4. electing the President, members of the Executive Board, the Audit Committee and the Disciplinary Court, and reviewing and approving reports on their activities;
  5. granting discharge to the outgoing Executive Board upon a motion of the Audit Committee;
  6. adopting regulations defining the modes of operation of the bodies of the APM;
  7. considering appeals against decisions and rulings of the governing bodies of the APM;
  8. adopting resolutions concerning honorary membership of the APM;
  9. determining annual membership fees;
  10. adopting resolutions on all other matters included in the agenda.

§ 23

  1. The Ordinary General Meeting of Members shall be convened by the Executive Board at least once every four years, upon expiry of the term of office of the governing bodies of the APM. Members shall be notified of the date, venue and agenda at least one month in advance.
  2. The General Meeting of Members may be held remotely using electronic means of communication that ensure:
    1. real-time transmission of proceedings between participants;
    2. two-way real-time communication enabling members to participate in discussions during the meeting;
    3. the personal exercise of voting rights during the meeting.
  3. In the case referred to in paragraph 2, the notice of the meeting shall contain a detailed description of the method of participation and voting.
  4. The following persons may participate in the General Meeting of Members:
    1. with voting rights:
      • ordinary members;
      • honorary members.
    2. with advisory voice:
      • representatives of supporting members;
      • invited guests.
  5. The proceedings of the General Meeting of Members shall be chaired by a chairperson elected from among its participants.
  6. If the quorum required at a meeting referred to in paragraphs 1 and 2 is not present, the chairperson, after consultation with the secretary, shall set a second meeting on the same day, 15 minutes after the first scheduled time. Resolutions adopted at such a meeting shall be valid regardless of the number of members present and entitled to vote.

§ 24

  1. An Extraordinary General Meeting of Members may be convened by the Executive Board on its own initiative, at the request of the Audit Committee, or upon demand of one-third of all members of the APM.
  2. The applicant shall determine the date, venue and agenda of the Extraordinary General Meeting of Members and notify members at least two weeks in advance. The meeting shall deal exclusively with the matters for which it was convened.
  3. The provisions of §23 paragraphs 2–6 shall apply accordingly.

B. Executive Board

§ 25

The Executive Board shall be the highest governing body of the APM between meetings of the General Meeting of Members.

§ 26

  1. The Executive Board shall direct the activities of the APM in accordance with resolutions of the General Meeting of Members and shall represent the APM externally and act on its behalf.
  2. The responsibilities of the Executive Board shall include in particular:
    1. implementing the objectives of the Association;
    2. establishing detailed directions of the APM’s activities on the basis of resolutions of the General Meeting of Members;
    3. adopting the APM budget and approving financial reports for presentation to the General Meeting of Members;
    4. maintaining records of members and membership fees;
    5. submitting motions concerning honorary membership;
    6. adopting resolutions on the accession of the APM to national and international organisations and appointing its representatives thereto;
    7. managing the assets of the APM;
    8. convening the General Meeting of Members;
    9. organising and supervising the statutory economic and publishing activities of the APM;
    10. granting distinctions and awards of the APM.

§ 27

  1. The Executive Board shall consist of 5–6 persons, including the President, Vice-President, Secretary and Treasurer. The Editor-in-Chief of Folia Malacologica, or a designated representative, shall participate in the work of the Executive Board, particularly in matters concerning the journal.
  2. The Executive Board shall be chaired by the President, and in the President’s absence or upon authorisation, by the Vice-President. The President shall convene meetings of the Executive Board at least once a year. The provisions of §23 paragraphs 2–6 shall apply accordingly to meetings of the Executive Board.
  3. The Secretary of the Executive Board shall maintain the records of the APM and supervise compliance of its activities with the Statute.
  4. The Treasurer of the Executive Board shall maintain records concerning the assets of the APM and its membership register.

C. Audit Committee

§ 28

The Audit Committee shall be the supervisory body of the APM.

§ 29

  1. The duties of the Audit Committee shall include:
    1. supervising all activities of the APM, with particular regard to its scientific, financial and economic activities in terms of their purposefulness and reliability;
    2. presenting reports and motions to the General Meeting of Members;
    3. proposing approval of reports of the Executive Board and granting discharge to the outgoing Executive Board;
    4. submitting post-audit recommendations and observations to the Executive Board and requesting explanations where necessary.
  2. The Audit Committee shall consist of three members, including a Chair. Members of the Committee may not hold any other office within the governing bodies of the APM.
  3. Meetings of the Audit Committee shall be held at least once a year. The provisions of §23 paragraphs 2–6 shall apply accordingly.
  4. Members of the Audit Committee shall have the right to participate, in an advisory capacity, in meetings of the Executive Board and all scientific bodies of the APM.

D. Disciplinary Court

§ 30

  1. The Disciplinary Court shall consider cases concerning violations of the Statute by members of the APM.
  2. The Disciplinary Court may impose the following sanctions:
    1. reprimand;
    2. expulsion from the register of members of the APM.
  3. Decisions of the Disciplinary Court may be appealed to the next General Meeting of Members.

§ 31

  1. The Disciplinary Court shall consist of three members and shall be chaired by its Chairperson.
  2. The procedure of the Disciplinary Court shall be governed by regulations adopted by the Court and approved by the Executive Board. The provisions of §23 paragraphs 2–6 shall apply accordingly to meetings of the Court.

§ 32

The assets of the APM shall consist of real estate, movable property and funds.

§ 33

The assets of the APM shall derive from:

  1. admission fees, membership fees and contributions from supporting members;
  2. domestic and foreign donations, inheritances and bequests;
  3. income from the statutory activities of the APM;
  4. income derived from real estate and movable property used by the APM;
  5. economic activities conducted for the purpose of achieving the objectives of the APM;
  6. income from publications and conferences organised for the Association as a whole;
  7. governmental and non-governmental grants and subsidies;
  8. public fundraising activities.

§ 34

  1. The assets of the APM shall be administered by the Executive Board.
  2. The conclusion of contracts, granting of powers of attorney, making declarations of intent concerning the assets of the APM, and signing financial documents, accounts and cheques shall require the joint action of the President and the Treasurer of the Executive Board, or two other members of the Executive Board authorised by it.

§ 35

A resolution to amend the Statute shall be adopted by the General Meeting of Members of the APM by a two-thirds majority of votes cast in the presence of at least one-half of the statutory membership. The provisions of §23 paragraph 6 shall apply accordingly.

§ 36

  1. A resolution on the dissolution of the APM shall be adopted by the General Meeting of Members by a two-thirds majority of votes cast by members of the APM present and entitled to vote.
  2. In the event of a resolution to dissolve the Association, the General Meeting of Members shall appoint a Liquidation Committee, adopt regulations governing its activities, and decide on the disposal of the assets of the APM.
